Lessons

Lesson 1: Excuse me!

Excuse me! Yes? Is this your handbag? Pardon? Is this your handbag? Yes, it is. Thank you very much.

对不起！什么事？这是您的手提包吗？对不起？这是您的手提包吗？是的，是的。非常感谢。

Lesson 2: Is this your...?

Is this your pencil? Yes, it is. It's my pencil. Is this your book? No, it isn't. It's not my book.

这是您的铅笔吗？是的，是的。这是我的铅笔。这是您的书吗？不，不是的。这不是我的书。
